Protect yourself

  • Never share your M-PESA PIN, OTP or ID number. Staff never ask for it.
  • Only trust warnings that come from your village SMS number or a known elder or scout.
  • Report a fake alert by sending SCAM#YourVillage#what you got.
  • Do not click short links in unexpected messages.
  • Use a screen lock and keep your phone updated.
  • Staff: use a unique password and two-step sign-in.
Community Safe is a coordination aid, not a replacement for national emergency services. In life-threatening danger call 999 or 112.
